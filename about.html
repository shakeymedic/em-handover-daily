What this is

  • One teaching point, one safety alert and one question, refreshed daily.
  • Sized to be read from across the department on a large screen.
  • No login, no accounts, no analytics, no user data collected.
  • Works offline once loaded, so a dropped hospital network does not empty the screen.

Disclaimer. Educational resource only. Not a substitute for clinical judgement. Always refer to local guidelines and current BNF/NICE guidance.

Sources. Clinical content cites a primary source. Safety alerts link directly to MHRA or RCEM rather than being paraphrased, because a paraphrase is not a safe basis for a clinical decision.

Drafts. Modules marked Draft have not been through clinical sign-off. Check them against your local guideline before teaching from them.

Corrections. If something here is wrong, it matters. Raise an issue on the repository or tell the editor directly.

Running it at handover

  • Open the site on the department screen and press h for handover mode.
  • The page reloads itself just after midnight, so the screen rolls to the new day.
  • Screen 2 holds the deeper dive and the question — three minutes at most.
  • Print gives a single A4 sheet for the noticeboard.
