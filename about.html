What this is

  • One teaching point, one safety alert, and one paper — refreshed daily at handover.
  • The handover page follows the three-section ED structure. No text to fill in — read aloud as a prompt.
  • Active department trials shown on the teaching and handover pages.
  • No login, no accounts, no analytics, no user data collected.
  • Works offline once loaded — a dropped hospital network does not empty the screen.

Disclaimer. Educational resource only. Not a substitute for clinical judgement. Always refer to local guidelines and current BNF/NICE guidance.

Sources. Clinical content cites a primary source. Safety alerts link directly to MHRA or RCEM rather than being paraphrased.

Drafts. Modules marked Draft have not been through clinical sign-off. Check against your local guideline before teaching from them.

Corrections. If something is wrong, raise an issue on the repository or contact the editor directly.

Running it at handover

  • Open on the department screen — press h or the button for handover mode.
  • The handover page has three colour-coded sections: blue (all staff), teal (resus/education/paeds), amber (split teams).
  • The page reloads itself just after midnight so the screen rolls to the new day.
  • Teaching page: Screen 2 holds the deep dive and the SBA question — three minutes at most.
  • Print gives a clean A4 sheet for the noticeboard.
